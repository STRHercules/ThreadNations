# ThreadNations
A living, breathing simulation based on your codex/claude activity.

## Core Concept

Imagine a game called **Thread Nations**.

The game runs permanently in a transparent desktop window or wallpaper layer.

Every active AI conversation creates and influences a civilization.

### AI Providers Become Nations

* Codex = Engineering-focused nation
* Claude = Research-focused nation
* ChatGPT = Generalist nation
* Gemini = Industrial nation

Or alternatively:

Each conversation thread becomes its own nation.

A "Stardew Valley Mod Discussion" thread becomes a farming kingdom.

A "Rust MMO Design" thread becomes a military-industrial state.

A "Tax Question" thread becomes a bureaucracy-heavy republic.

---

## Activity Drives Civilization Growth

### Tokens = Labor

Every token consumed generates economic activity.

Example:

1000 tokens spent:

* 100 Workers created
* 25 tons Iron mined
* 15 Tools manufactured
* 5 Houses constructed

Large coding sessions create huge industrial booms.

---

### Conversation Length = Civilization Age

New thread:

* Primitive settlement
* Wood huts
* Basic farming

100k token thread:

* Large cities
* Railroads
* Universities

1 million token mega-thread:

* Nation-state
* Massive armies
* Advanced technology

## Claude/Codex Integration

You don't need official APIs initially.

You can monitor:

* Browser tabs
* Window titles
* Local exported conversations
* Token reports
* Activity duration

Metrics:

```text
Codex:
  1.2M tokens today

Claude:
  800k tokens today

ChatGPT:
  300k tokens today
```

These become economic inputs.

---

## Military System

Economic power eventually creates conflict.

Example:

Nation Alpha:

* High technology
* Small population

Nation Beta:

* Massive population
* Weak industry

War emerges over:

* Iron
* Oil
* Rare resources
* Strategic ports

Battles resolve automatically.

You are not controlling units.

You are watching history unfold.

Think:

* Dwarf Fortress
* Victoria 3
* The Political Process

combined into a desktop companion.

---

## Desktop Integration

The coolest version would live directly on the desktop.

### Layer 1

Tiny settlements appear on unused desktop space.

### Layer 2

Roads connect cities.

### Layer 3

Trade ships travel between nations.

### Layer 4

Wars visibly happen.

The desktop slowly becomes a living world.

The more AI work you do, the more active the world becomes.

## Technical Architecture (Rust)

Rust is actually a great fit.

```text
desktop-client/
├── renderer/
│   ├── egui
│   ├── wgpu
│   └── transparent-overlay
│
├── simulation/
│   ├── population
│   ├── economy
│   ├── military
│   ├── diplomacy
│   └── technology
│
├── integrations/
│   ├── codex
│   ├── claude
│   ├── chatgpt
│   └── browser_monitor
│
├── persistence/
│   ├── sqlite
│   └── world_state
│
└── desktop_overlay/
```

For simulation scale:

* ECS via [Bevy Engine](https://bevyengine.org?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* Rendering via [wgpu](https://wgpu.rs?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* Storage via [SQLite](https://www.sqlite.org?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The most compelling version isn't a traditional game at all.

It's a **persistent civilization ecosystem generated from your thinking activity**, where every AI conversation literally becomes part of world history.
